## rps-deck

Building slides for a university session that has an approved RPS? Use the toolkit at
`~/.agent-tools/rps-deck/` — read its `CONTEXT.md` and `SKILL.md` first. Six steps:
reconcile the RPS against the session note, refit to the real slot, sanitise what the
session projects, author a reviewable spec, build naskah and materi separately, publish the
materi only.
